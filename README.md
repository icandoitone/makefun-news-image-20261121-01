A newspaper-front-page image summing up the world's news of
**Saturday 21 November 2026**, built for the
[make.fun bounty "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121).

## What the image shows

The front page of "The World Today", dated Saturday 21 November 2026:

- **Lead story — The Hague:** the World Court finds Myanmar committed
  genocide against the Rohingya, the first time an international court has
  found a state guilty of genocide.
- **Poland / Ukraine:** Russian missiles land in Poland; NATO calls it a grave
  violation of its airspace, as fresh strikes hit Ukraine — two killed and
  42 hurt in Kryvyi Rih.
- **India / Weather:** Cyclone Akvan crosses the Odisha coast near Gopalpur
  with strong winds and heavy rain.
- **Turkey / Climate:** COP31 climate talks enter their final stage, with
  negotiators haggling over fossil fuels and climate finance.
- **Washington / Tech:** the US approved about 900,000 AI chip exports to
  China, a House panel discloses.
- **Also today:** Gaza aid appeal by Australia, Canada, Italy and the UK; a
  Russian military transport plane crash in Siberia kills all 19 aboard;
  cyberattacks on vendors hit JPMorgan, Visa and Mastercard; a fourth weekend
  of cost-of-living protests in France.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies the
pre-rendered `entry.png` (1024 × 1024, under 1 MiB) to `/output/entry.png`.
Running it twice writes the exact same bytes.
